# Contributing

For a Reason is non-profit and open source, improved by the people who use it.

- **Found a content mistake?** Use "Report a mistake" in the app. It opens a prefilled issue with the item ID.
- **Native or near-native speaker?** Review items in `src/data_*.js` and `src/tracks.js` and open a PR.
- **Want a new language or reason track?** Open a Discussion first, then follow "Adding a language" in the README.
- **Code:** edit files in `src/`, rebuild with `cd src && python3 build.py && python3 about.py`, and open a PR.

Profiles are self-declared. We don't build verification, badges for sale or leaderboards that reward lying. Be kind; people here are learning a language for a reason.
